## Affected Pages/Routes

- Inbound PSTN call control — verify the new Bridge profile is source-only and requires explicit `cedar` configuration.
- Realtime phone session — verify model, low reasoning, 500 ms VAD, full audio allowance, official-answer tool, and prompt budget remain unchanged.
- Phone greeting — verify the required English automated-service disclosure, direct identity answers, and reviewed Spanish speech after language detection.
- Deterministic call controls — verify `no thanks` does not hang up; explicit stop/end requests do; quoted, mentioned, and negated commands do not trigger effects.

## Key Interactions to Verify

- Ordinary caller statement receives one to three short sentences and at most one question.
- Off-topic conversation is answered first, followed by a permissioned property-tax bridge; a decline is respected without ending the call.
- Current or official Wayne County facts still invoke the approved lookup path.
- Private detail uses the secure path; person, link, and end requests retain deterministic effects.
- Missing voice configuration stays on `marin`; explicit candidate configuration selects `cedar`; renderer behavior remains unchanged.

## Edge Cases

- Caller says “no thanks,” “stop,” “please do not hang up,” or quotes someone saying “goodbye.”
- Caller asks whether Civya is human, Maya, a friend, a county employee, or remembers them.
- Caller changes topic, declines a bridge, returns later, or mentions a consequential medical/legal/financial/safety issue.
- Noisy or accented speech, silence, interruption, incomplete speech, provider lookup failure, and repeat calls.
- Spanish request after the English opening; qualified bilingual review of subsequent approved copy.

## Critical Paths

- Harbor refs resolve to the frozen commit and the release record restores `marin` plus the stable configuration.
- Bridge source builds and all phone/profile/official-answer regression suites pass.
- Candidate configuration tuple is exact: source commit, `phone_fast`, profile `civya-bridge-v2-2026-07-20`, model `gpt-realtime-2.1`, voice `cedar`, low reasoning, 500 ms VAD, transcription `gpt-4o-transcribe`.
- Before activation: blind staged PSTN audio, latency p50/p95, complete-sentence evidence, representative dignity research, privacy evidence, non-AI fallback, bilingual review, and named R3 approvals all pass.
